# Privacy Policy — Bellas Bus Uploader

Bellas Bus Uploader is a private automation tool used by the owner of the Bellas Bus Buddies YouTube channel to upload videos to their own channel using the YouTube Data API.

It does not collect, store, or share data from anyone else. It only accesses the YouTube account authorized by the channel owner.

No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.
